# Forming SIP chart preview

The 15 November 2026 implementation uses the Alpaca SIP **minute** rules for CTA tapes A and B and Nasdaq tape C. The table below groups the published condition codes by the fields they affect. A literal space is the regular-sale code for tapes A and B. All conditions must be recognized for their tape and the strictest rule applies independently to each field. A volume-only print cannot establish a price. A forming minute needs positive eligible volume and all four prices before it can be plotted. [Alpaca Market Data FAQ, How are bars aggregated?](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq#how-are-bars-aggregated)

| SIP minute fields | Tapes A and B | Tape C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open, high, low, close and volume | space, E, F, K, L, O, T, X, 5, 6 | @, A, B, D, F, K, L, O, T, X, Y, 5, 6 |
| Volume only | B, C, H, I, N, P, R, U, V, Z, 4, 7 | C, G, H, I, N, P, R, U, V, W, Z, 4, 7 |
| No field | M, Q, 9 | M, Q, 9 |

The existing trade subscription includes correction and cancel/error channels. Forming coverage requires explicit acknowledgement of all three channels, a warm and reconciled symbol in the active connection, and the first full minute after both conditions hold. The same rule applies after a coverage loss or repair. Event time assigns a minute and retains all nine available fractional digits. Tape, exchange and original provider ID give a deterministic order when event times are identical. The receipt clock is taken when the WebSocket message is assembled, before decoding the messages within it. [Alpaca Real-time Stock Data, trade schemas and subscription acknowledgement](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data)

A correction matches the retained tape, exchange, ID, original price, size and conditions and recalculates the affected eligible fields at the original event position. A cancel or error removes the same identifiable print using its retained identity, price and size. Alpaca supplies a message timestamp for both mutations but does not identify the original event time separately. When an original cannot be proved, the current preview clears and any potentially affected earlier child in a larger bucket needs a new canonical receipt. Known late prints identify their child by event time. An exact duplicate in the retained minute changes neither prices nor observation clocks. [Alpaca Real-time Stock Data, Trade Corrections and Trade Cancels/Errors](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data#trade-corrections)

The chart combines eligible current trades with the existing accepted canonical children for 5m and 15m buckets. Earlier children require a confirmed native bar or explicit canonical proof of no qualifying trades. A verified session end clips the bucket. The ledger is held for the open minute and bounded at 10,000 events per symbol and 100,000 per connection. The same SignalR watch publishes a separate preview frame after its completed snapshot or heartbeat, with a 2 KiB serialized frame limit. The completed snapshot and history cursor keep their existing keys and reads.

The offline cases cover tape-specific eligibility, out-of-order event clocks, exact duplicates, conflicting identities, corrections, cancellations, quiet and unknown children, canonical replacement, early segment close, missing or invalid schedules and verified closure, lease and generation changes, overflow, completed-output parity, side-frame order, and browser selection and paging. The isolated receive-loop fixture checks existing trade and halt state, the upstream command list, and rejection of a saved canonical copy when the processor has detected a repairable gap. Live acceptance is pending an already authorized, attended SIP session. It requires matching the eligible captured trade receipt to a preview and the canonical receipt to the resulting completed candle.
