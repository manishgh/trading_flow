# TradingFlow user guide

TradingFlow is a local workspace for following US stocks, testing trading rules against historical data, and monitoring authorized orders in an Alpaca paper account. Paper orders use simulated money. Use the Desk to see what the system knows about a stock and Strategy Lab to see how a defined rule behaved in a test.

## Sign in and check the workspace

1. Open the local app and choose **Sign in**. Use the **Username** and **Password** for the operator account provisioned on your server. Use **Keep me signed in on this device** only on a device you control. [Open TradingFlow](http://127.0.0.1:10088)
2. On **Desk**, check the **Paper** badge, workspace status strip, and **NY**, **Local** (browser time zone; Berlin on this workstation), and **UTC** clocks. The strip separates trading permission, market session and feed operation; expand **Source freshness** to inspect current source observations. An active feed does not guarantee fresh coverage for every stock. The exchange calendar and trading sessions follow New York time. Backtest date fields marked **UTC** use UTC. **Live** is locked.
3. Open **Settings → Health** to reach **Operations**. Look at **Market session**, **Broker sync**, **Entry gate**, and **Subsystems**. The entry gate is the server's permission to open new positions. An open gate still leaves each proposed order subject to its own checks.

Read the workspace status strip for the current trading state. You can use Desk, charts, News, Earnings, and research while trading is paused. Paper submission requires an enabled trading runtime and the applicable authorization; opening Buy or Sell for review does not grant that authorization. News and Earnings are also available before sign-in. Submission, fills, protective orders and exits have not yet been exercised during an open market on this build. A running paper host is not evidence that execution has passed that separate check.

## Organize stocks and save a Finviz screen

1. Open **Stock groups**. To make a wishlist, enter a name under **New stock group** and click **Add new**. Select that group, paste ticker symbols in **Add stocks**, and click **Add**. A ticker is the exchange symbol for a stock.
2. To capture the current matches from a Finviz screen in the selected group, open **Import from Finviz**, choose a saved screen or paste its URL or filter query, and click **Import**. Check the resulting **Stocks** list.
3. To reuse the screen itself, open **Saved Finviz screens**, give the query a name, paste it in **Finviz URL or filter query**, and click **Save screen**. You can also save one from Desk with **+ Saved Elite screen**. A saved screen keeps its query for later use, while an import adds its current results to a group.
4. Click **Open on Desk** from the group, or use **Stock group** on Desk to choose a group, a saved screen, or **All followed stocks**.

Groups and screens discover which stocks to examine. Today’s list contains today’s members. A historical test using those same members is diagnostic because it cannot establish which stocks would have met the screen on each past date. [Operating boundaries](operating-boundaries.md#universe-boundary)

Open **Scheduled Finviz capture** under **Saved Finviz screens** or **Settings → Health**. When enabled, the collector observes each saved screen 30 minutes before the regular exchange open and 15 minutes after its actual close. A typical session has slots at 09:00 and 16:15 New York time. Early closes move the second slot, and holidays have no slots. Read **Next slot** and receipt times in New York, Berlin, and UTC, since the time differences can shift around daylight-saving changes.

Compare **Latest slot** with **Last capture received**. A missed or failed slot can leave an earlier receipt as the latest available capture. Read any partial-coverage or unknown-revision reason alongside the receipt. CSV rows are records returned by Finviz. When matching-row membership is unverified, even a CSV with headers and zero rows leaves the matching list unknown. Scheduled observations can run while trading is paused and do not add stocks to a group or grant trading permission. [Scheduled capture design](design-review/2026-11-15/fable-remaining-work.md#next-1-start-retaining-scheduled-finviz-observations)

## Prepare a list for the next session

When the local app is paused, you can inspect saved workflows, save drafts, and review previously collected evidence. Wait until the preparation worker is enabled before scheduling or requesting a new collection, including a curated revision.

1. Start with **Today’s preparation** above the Desk stock list. It shows the target New York session, saved evidence expiry, worker state, and evening → morning → review → Desk steps. Follow **Review saved evidence**, or select **Preparation** in the main navigation. Choose a **Workflow** and click **Open**, or choose **New workflow** to create one. [Open Morning preparation directly](http://127.0.0.1:10088/Preparation)
2. In **Workflow settings**, name it and add a **Stock group** or a **Finviz screen**. If you add several sources, **In any chosen source** keeps the union and **In every chosen source** keeps the intersection. Review **Schedule and filters** in New York time. Click **Save paused workflow** for a new draft or **Save settings** for an existing workflow. When the worker is enabled, review **Upcoming exchange sessions** before using **Enable workflow** for scheduled collection.
3. With the worker enabled, open **Schedule and manual collection**. While the workflow is idle and the matching exchange-session window is open, use **Run evening collection now** or **Run morning collection now**. In **Latest review**, verify the date, status, and changes from the evening result. If the evening list says **Collect and review the morning result before continuing**, open the morning result when available and review it within its displayed validity window. Use **Refresh** to load a completed revision and **History** to inspect prior results.
4. In **Collected stock list**, open the evidence under each ticker. Check price history, the feed and adjustment basis, and any missing inputs. **Gap** is the change from the prior reference close. **Same-time RVOL** compares volume with the usual volume at that point in the session. Keep the complete rows you intend to use. With the worker enabled, gather evidence for added tickers through **Adjust reviewed membership → Collect curated revision**.
5. Under **Continue with this list**, record **Why these stocks**. **Review and open on Desk** records the accepted morning revision and opens its stocks for viewing; this action does not create a candidate run or order. Desk displays the exact reviewed source, revision and expiry. To request strategy checks separately, select a **Research strategy** and use **Research these stocks** when offered. **Start a paper run with them** opens paper setup only when the reviewed list is eligible. The strategy still evaluates each stock and paper authorization still applies.

Check **News** and **Earnings** before the session. In **News**, use **Stock source**, **Window**, and **Ticker** or **Search**, then click **Apply**. The matching count can exceed the stories on the current page. The source-status heading distinguishes a pending first refresh, current success, partial results and failure while preserving stored stories. In **Earnings**, choose **Next reporting day** or **This week · NY**, and check the analysis state, time and **Evidence window**. Pending or failed analysis cannot establish that no events exist. Earnings lanes and news classifications provide context for research and review.

## Inspect a stock and its chart

1. On **Desk**, choose a **Stock group**, then use **Find a stock** or select the ticker in the table. Choose a **Strategy** to inspect its checks when one is available. Use **Refresh strategy checks** if offered. **All**, **Signals**, **Held**, and **Rejected** narrow the table. Check the quote time and the displayed reason before interpreting a signal or rejection.
2. Read the stock row's positive/negative news counts, completed daily technicals, RVOL and volume. Missing observations remain unavailable instead of displaying zero. On desktop, news stays beside the stock table and the selected chart opens in the same workspace. **Buy** and **Sell** open the shared review ticket; they do not submit an order. Any paused-runtime, account or authorization refusal is explained by the ticket. Additional holding and strategy evidence stays available in the selected-stock detail.
3. Open the chart from a stock link, or use the embedded **Price & volume** chart on Desk. Set **Candle interval** to **1 minute**, **5 minutes**, **15 minutes**, **1 day**, or **1 week**. The chart requests completed Alpaca SIP candles when opened. On intraday views, **Regular** shows the regular session and **Extended** adds premarket and after-hours data where available. Watching extended hours leaves order permissions unchanged.
4. Use **Load earlier**, **Go to latest**, or the arrow keys on the chart to inspect candles. The readout below the plot keeps the selected candle’s date or time, open, high, low, close and volume together. Intraday candles show New York and Berlin time and the session. Daily candles show the exchange session date. Weekly candles show the dates of their first and last exchange sessions. **Back to Desk**, or **Desk** in the main navigation, restores this tab's stock selection and view preferences. Quotes and account information are read again; no order is reopened and no fresh strategy assessment is started by restoring the view. Use **Refresh strategy checks** to request a fresh assessment.
5. Open **Chart details** beside the chart navigation. **Indicator values** shows the readings for the selected candle. **Data evidence** shows feed, price basis, freshness and coverage, plus the selected candle’s start, end, source, original receipt, retrieval and availability when provided. It also shows the indicator values’ availability, calculation start, observation time and full source revisions when recorded. **Layers & price levels** contains display toggles, confirmed zones, reference levels and selected-candle comparisons. **News & earnings** lists stored events. Use **Close** or press Esc to return to the chart.
6. **Structure source → Intraday · 15m** uses confirmed fifteen-minute price turns. **Daily · 1d** exposes **Daily lookback** choices of 64 sessions, week, month or year while preserving your candle interval. For example, keep five-minute candles and select a year of daily pivots. The chart shows up to two nearest support zones and two nearest resistance zones for that source and lookback. The current close can be inside a zone. Current labelled bands sit at the right edge to mark current structure. Where evidence is available, **Layers & price levels** shows previous-day/week levels, the opening fifteen-minute range and premarket levels. A support zone describes an observed price area whose future behavior is uncertain.
7. Use **Indicator preset** for a starting display. **Intraday** adds session VWAP, EMA20 and an ATR readout in **Chart details**. **Swing · 1d** switches to daily candles and adds daily SMA50/200, RSI14 and ATR. Use **Indicator library** to add or replace studies, then remove individual selections when no longer useful. Preferences stay in this browser. **Reset chart settings** restores the base view.

| Indicator group | Available choices | Reading the display |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trend | SMA20/50/200; EMA9/20/50/200 | Price lines. Choose candle interval or daily source for moving averages. EMA20 on 5m means twenty five-minute observations. |
| Momentum | RSI14; MACD12/26/9; ADX14 with +DI/−DI | Separate panes; descriptive references do not authorize a trade. |
| Volatility | ATR14; ATR percent; Bollinger20/2 | ATR as readout or pane; bands on price. |
| Volume | Volume bars; RTH VWAP; OBV; same-time RVOL | VWAP resets at the New York regular open. RVOL needs twenty verified prior sessions at the same completed session time. |

At most four price studies and two lower panes fit at once; ATR and OBV count toward the pane limit. On a phone, use **Visible indicator pane** to switch between the selected lower panes. An unavailable or partial study states the missing evidence instead of drawing guessed values. A cold fifteen-session download cannot establish the twenty-session RVOL baseline.

Recursive indicators need warmed history and fixed seed anchors. The visible suffix can therefore be shorter than the candles. Gaps or separate anchored segments break the line; OBV starts again from zero at each segment anchor. Loading earlier candles does not change the values already displayed. Reconstructed history is labelled as reconstructed, with actual receipt times: daily data downloaded today cannot appear as if known on an earlier intraday candle. [Calculation and availability details](design-review/2026-11-15/chart-indicator-availability.md)

The chart keeps completed candles and stored market events as its main series. Weekly candles use completed exchange weeks built from adjusted SIP daily candles. Weekly indicators, price levels, session shading and private evidence are unavailable. Volume profile remains deferred because ordinary candles do not show how much volume traded at each price. The chart reads existing data and uses history refresh for stocks outside the active trading stream. When completed data is available, a history-backed open-session chart, native daily chart or weekly chart says **History only**. A closed intraday chart can say **Market closed**. The browser connection itself is a WebSocket. Four concurrent chart connections per operator are supported.

### Optional forming candles and paper evidence

On a one-, five- or fifteen-minute chart, open **Chart details → Layers & price levels → Forming candle preview**. When the stock already has a verified active SIP trade stream, an outlined **Forming · SIP** candle shows the unfinished interval. Its status and source clocks are separate from the selected completed candle. A reconnect, unknown trade condition, unresolved correction or incomplete interval can make the preview unavailable until coverage is proved again. Opening a chart does not subscribe another stock to the upstream feed. Indicators, zones and strategy decisions continue to use completed candles.

On a permitted Paper run or Orders row, **Paper chart** opens a private chart for that exact order or position. The host must grant the signed-in administrator access to that exact paper context; the default grant list is empty. In **Chart details**, use **Paper order evidence** to inspect recorded decisions, fills and protection. Zoom to a completed interval of no more than 31 days. Markers show verified event times; raw broker prices remain in details with **Price overlay unavailable** until their adjustment basis can be matched to the market chart. Planned protection is distinct from verified broker protection. Missing or incomplete evidence is stated explicitly. A normal stock link, including Desk, continues to show market data only. Closing the private chart or changing context discards its private display data.

These optional layers have synthetic and browser verification. Matching an actual forming trade to its subsequent official candle, and reconciling private markers against an authorized paper fill, remain separate acceptance observations. [Private access settings and verification](verification/private-paper-chart-2026-11-15.md) · [Forming source rules and verification](verification/forming-chart-preview-2026-11-15.md)

## Design and start a backtest

1. Open **Strategies → Backtests** to reach **Strategy Lab**. **Configure backtest** proceeds through rule and horizon, stocks and dates, capital and execution assumptions, then review and save. Select strategies to compare and open **Identity & assumptions** to check each version and content hash. Review **Capital and cost profile** and **Simulated fill policy**, including fill timing. Advanced settings remain available in their disclosures. Use one strategy horizon per saved plan. A horizon describes the intended holding period, such as same-day or multi-day.
2. Name the run. Enter **From UTC** and **To UTC**, or set **Fallback lookback (days)**. Use dates that reflect the experiment you planned. Keep completed indicator warm-up history before the test window, and review any minimum history or liquidity filter. For a study meant to validate a rule, write down its universe, test dates, cost assumptions, and an untouched test period before looking at results.
3. Review **Starting capital (USD)**, **Account risk (% of equity)**, **Max position notional (% of equity)**, and **Max concurrent positions (count)**. Account risk limits the planned loss at the stop. Notional is shares times price and limits the size of the holding. In a hypothetical example, 50 shares at $100 have $5,000 notional. A $98 stop implies $100 planned loss before fees and any execution gap.
4. Use **Stock groups and exclusions** if you need to exclude symbols or compare separate batches. Click **Preview groups** and verify the exact stocks, per-group capital, dates, strategies, and assumptions. Click **Save plan**, pick it in **Saved plans**, then click **Start saved plan**. If you change inputs before saving, preview again.
5. Monitor **Running**. The stage and per-strategy work counts show whether the run is preparing candles, evaluating stocks, or writing results. Open **Prepared candles** when available to check the provider, feed, price basis, requested UTC coverage, and snapshot ID. Use **Recent runs** to return to a run or **Cancel run** if you intend to stop it.

A completed bar is the full price and volume observation for its interval. TradingFlow requires decisions to use completed information and models fills later than the signal. With a daily candle alone, a simulator may be unable to tell whether an entry or stop came first. Use documented five-minute execution from the fill to judge a protective-stop strategy, and read finer timing or liquidity limitations noted for the replay. Missing execution evidence can make results unavailable. [Research operating rules](research/research-operations-runbook.md)

For a concrete comparison, the existing saved plan 192 compares the monthly ETF trend rule with its buy-and-hold control on the same sector-fund universe and dates. Open that existing result and choose the same source-session month for both strategies. Read each strategy's separate capital, costs, positions and decision. This explains a comparison without starting a new test or reactivating the deferred rotation strategy.
## Read monthly results and costs

1. Open a completed run and find **Monthly strategy returns**. Choose a **Source-session month** and click **Show month**. Compare one strategy, exact version, and stock group at a time. The month follows New York exchange-session dates. The return divides the change in equity by that month’s opening equity. Keep **partial month** rows identified when comparing them.
2. Read the valuation label. **Daily close equity, including open positions** values positions still held using the reported close. **Realized closed-trade equity** reflects closed-trade results and fees without price changes on open positions. Check monthly drawdown, fees, exposure, and open and closed counts. Drawdown measures the drop from a prior equity high. Treat **unavailable** as missing evidence and read its reason.
3. Open a strategy row to inspect **Trades**, rejection reasons, price history eligibility, and **Promotion gates** when shown. A fill is an executed quantity. A trade can require several fills across entry and exit. Check the recorded fees and the run’s assumptions for spread, slippage, trading volume limits, market impact, and short borrow. Spread is the gap between the highest bid and lowest offer. Slippage is the difference between the reference and modeled execution prices. A short sale depends on shares being available to borrow.
4. When **Monthly benchmark** is shown, compare the same month and read its price basis. A price-only benchmark measures price movement and omits dividends. It may also omit modeled trading costs. Check that the comparison covers the same dates, and read exposure and drawdown alongside it. An unavailable benchmark cannot establish relative performance.

Read **Research assessment from retained evidence** and the selected run’s evidence gates. That assessment is derived from the currently retained summary; it is not a newly persisted promotion decision. For example, the retained plan 192 assessment can say `FAILED_GATE`. Review which requirements failed or still lack evidence. **Completed** describes job execution; a separate **Result unavailable** notice means the original artifact or its durable publication evidence cannot be verified. Retained monthly records can still be inspected, but do not substitute them for the missing complete artifact. Paper access requires separate authorization. Keep original results, plans, and evidence, including unsuccessful runs. Cleanup of economic-run intermediates requires the recorded review for that exact run. [Research and promotion rules](operating-boundaries.md#promotion-boundary), [Backtest retention](backtesting-research-retention.md)

## Check research attempts and admission

Open **Strategies → Research ledger**. **Registered trials** shows the strategy family, hypothesis, try number, exact definition and dataset identities, published results, and protected partition state. Older trial counts stay unknown where registrations are missing. **Unopened in this registry** applies to a declared, verified protected partition. **Starts and outcomes** shows admission, diagnostic replays, and recorded outcomes, including failures and cancellations.

If a research start is refused, read its recorded reason. A new portfolio family needs a reviewed successful signal study tied to the exact frozen definition and evidence before the first portfolio experiment. A diagnostic replay needs a verified link to the original plan or artifact, or an explicitly reviewed replay. The ledger reads registry metadata, links to the retirement records, and displays the investment success policy as **Draft, awaiting approval**. [Research admission design](design-review/2026-11-15/fable-remaining-work.md#next-4-record-and-show-research-attempts-before-running-them)

## Start an authorized paper run

**Paper experiment** runs an explicitly registered research experiment under its own identity. Its results cannot promote a strategy. **Paper shadow** requires review and authorization for the exact strategy version and content hash. Both modes can route to the paper broker when enabled. Same-day strategies remain backtest-only under the current operating boundary.

1. Open **Strategies → Strategy runs** and check **Authorization** and **Strategy**. **None available** means the chosen mode has no eligible strategy. The local app is currently paused and no strategy is authorized for a run. Stay in research or monitoring until authorization and runtime approval are in place.
2. Once authorized, choose **Stocks from** using **Stock group**, **Finviz screen**, or **Both**. Check the chosen source, **Exact strategy identity & research evidence**, and the account’s **New stocks a day** and **Open positions at once** limits.

   The current Web launch freezes the combined stock membership at launch. Editing a stock group or refreshing a Finviz screen later does not change that active run; use a fresh preview and start a new run through the normal strategy/account gates to select updated membership. Scheduled Finviz archiving records evidence but does not refresh a running strategy or start one. An explicit source-refresh option is proposed, not installed.

3. Check **Entry order**, **Expires**, and **Trade pre-market and after hours**. Production opening orders currently require a protected limit order, which specifies the worst entry price you will accept and can remain unfilled. Market openings and extended-hours openings are refused by the current server policy, even if a general settings control is visible. Give the run a **Run name**, then click **Start strategy run** when the server permits it.
4. Open the run from **Running and recent**. Check **Recent Events**, **Recent Strategy Evaluations**, **Live Broker Orders**, and **Live Portfolio (Positions)**. Select **Open Audit Analyzer** for the run’s recorded decisions. An **exit-only** notice means the runner can handle existing exposure while new entries are disabled.

For an approved experiment, open **Experiments → Register an experiment** when an eligible **Research strategy** is available. Review its **Version**. If the approval includes different parameters, use **Change settings (needs a new version)** and enter the approved version and values. Click **Register experiment** and check the resulting identity under **Paper experiment** before starting a run.

For a separately authorized manual paper ticket, **Trading → New order → Open ticket** opens the review workflow. Check the account, symbol, side, quantity, quote, limit, stop, target, session, and expiration shown for the proposed order. **Review order** requests server validation. Only **Confirm paper order**, when offered after a valid review, submits it. For a blocked or stale review, correct the reason and request a new review. [UI operator runbook](ui-operator-runbook.md)

## Read the paper fill comparison

Open a run from **Strategies → Strategy runs → Running and recent** and find **Paper fill comparison**. Read the decision price, modeled executable price, order limit, and admission bid and ask separately. A missing reference shows **Unavailable**. Compare the decision time, quote observation and provider times, local pre-POST attempt, and broker execution time in New York, Berlin, and UTC. The local stamp records an attempt just before the broker call. The admission quote was seen earlier, so check its age, the delay to the attempt, and any freshness warning before using it as a reference.

Expand **Order status and evidence coverage** to see unfilled orders and missing quote, timing, or fill records. **New York fill months** shows the run’s fills by exact strategy version and hash, with a row for each reference price. Each row reports the covered orders, fills and units, the fill-price average weighted by quantity, and the adverse distance in basis points. A higher fill price is adverse for a buy and a lower fill price is adverse for a sell. A partial order can contribute fills to multiple months according to each fill’s execution time. Check coverage before comparing rows. [Paper fill comparison design](design-review/2026-11-15/fable-remaining-work.md#next-3-explain-the-distance-between-a-decision-and-its-paper-fills)

## Monitor fills and review entry blocks

1. Open **Trading** and compare **Positions** with **Orders**. In Orders, inspect requested and filled quantities and the latest status using the client order ID. **Partially filled** means part of the requested quantity has executed. For an opening order, this may already have created exposure; an exit fill may have reduced it. **Cancel pending** means the broker has yet to confirm cancellation and a further fill is possible. Check the remaining position and its protective order after the broker’s final update.
2. If a new entry is refused, open **Settings → Health → Entry gate**. Record the exact code, detail, and timestamp. Check its corresponding **Subsystems** evidence. An ordinary strategy rejection concerns that stock or setup. A broker-sync, missing-coverage, risk, or recovery block can affect the whole paper account. Try a fresh review only after the evidence for the condition has changed.
3. For a loss-limit block, an administrator opens **Review paper account loss limits** from Operations. Compare the last broker observation, daily and weekly stops, observed peak, funding evidence, and unresolved opening orders. The limits use equity including open positions. The defaults measure a 2% daily loss from the verified prior session close, a 4% weekly loss from the previous New York week’s last verified close, and a 10% drop from the observed equity peak. Daily and weekly stops last through their exchange periods. The peak stop requires review.
4. When supported by verified evidence and authorized, choose **Reset observed peak after review** or **Rebaseline after a recorded transfer**, enter **Review findings and reason**, then click **Record reviewed reset**. A rebaseline requires verified daily and weekly reference closes after the transfer. The action leaves independent loss, opening-order, and reconciliation blocks in force. If the snapshot changed or the runtime is paused, the server refuses the action.

An entry-only stop prevents new entries while independently authorized exits and protection remain available under an enabled runtime. The ticket can still show its fields and the refusal reason; opening it does not bypass server checks. A global trading pause rejects all order and position changes, including exits and protection changes.

An entry block may trigger cancellation of standalone opening orders whose ownership is verified. A linked entry and its protection may be preserved for review while the remainder can still fill. Read the opening-order review and check both current exposure and exact resting stop coverage before treating it as resolved. [Paper loss limits](design-review/2026-11-14/account-loss-limits.md)

## Finish the session and troubleshoot

1. Near the exchange close, check **Trading** for working opening orders, partial fills, and positions you intend to carry. Verify the order status, remaining quantity, and protection. A **When cancelled** order can remain open beyond the session.
2. For an authorized run you intend to stop, open its detail and click **Cancel Runner** when available. Wait for the recorded status and review **Recent Events** and **Trading**. Stopping the runner leaves positions for separate review. Use the reviewed position workflow for an intended exit and verify the broker outcome. A linked group requiring review should be investigated without canceling its protection to clear a page warning.
3. Save the run name, plan or job ID, source-session date, and any unresolved order or entry-block code. In preparation, verify the next scheduled exchange session if the workflow is enabled. Use **Settings → Sign out** to end the browser session.

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Quotes stale or chart empty | Check the symbol, session, last completed candle and **Chart details → Data evidence**. Use **Retry chart** or **Retry connection** when offered. A closed market and an unavailable feed are different states. |
| Strategy says it is waiting for data | Read the stated history or evidence requirement. **Settings → Warm-up** shows the cache and recent warm-up runs. A fresh quote alone does not establish enough completed bars for the rule. |
| Recent run is completed but says Result unavailable | Read the specific missing-artifact reason and record the job ID. Locate the original artifact and verify its identity; the UI does not repair references or invent missing results. |
| Orders or positions say unknown | Check **Broker sync**, read time, and entry gate detail. Treat the exposure as unknown until fresh broker evidence and journal reconciliation agree. |
