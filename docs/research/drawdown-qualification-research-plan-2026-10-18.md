# Drawdown qualification research and implementation plan

Date: 18 October 2026. Status: proposed diagnostic research; no new strategy, execution run, or trading authorization is created by this plan.

The question is whether the information available before an entry can distinguish pullbacks suited to our existing swing strategies from declines those strategies should avoid. A large fall, an oversold RSI, and a later profitable rebound are three different observations. Only the first two can be available at the original decision. We will measure qualification separately from entry timing and exits, and test the cost of rejecting opportunities as carefully as the losses avoided.

## Research findings and their limits

| Evidence | Finding relevant to this study | Consequence for our design |
|---|---|---|
| [Da, Liu and Schaumburg, 2014](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/Reversal.pdf) | Their reversal study separates industry effects and fundamental cash-flow news from residual price changes. Returns unexplained by their fundamental proxies are more likely to reverse. | Separate market/sector moves and observed company events. A sector-adjusted return alone does not prove a decline was nonfundamental. Their monthly long-short portfolios are not our long-only, several-day strategy. |
| [Wesley Chan, 2001 working paper](https://users.nber.org/~confer/2001/bfs01/chan.pdf) | The study finds different subsequent patterns for large moves with identifiable news and without it; bad-news stocks show drift. | Test adverse-event context independently. Missing articles in our feed cannot establish that no news existed. The paper's monthly horizon does not establish a profitable five-day veto. |
| [Campbell, Grossman and Wang, 1993](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/CampbellGrossmanWang93.pdf) | Their model connects volume and reversals to compensation for absorbing noninformational selling pressure. | High volume is not automatically a rejection signal. Examine price response and event context; OHLCV cannot identify sellers' motives. |
| [Alvarez, Volume and Mean Reversion, 2022](https://alvarezquanttrading.com/blog/volume-and-mean-reversion/) | In his particular tests, a low-volume filter improved some trade statistics but removed many opportunities; he did not consider the extra rule worthwhile for his strategy. | Compare portfolio performance, exposure and rejected winners, not only average return among retained trades. This is practitioner evidence, not independent proof for our stocks. |
| [Alvarez, Mean Reversion Construction, 2017](https://alvarezquanttrading.com/blog/the-abcs-of-creating-a-mean-reversion-strategy-part1/) | Describes several overlapping ways to measure a pullback, including RSI, band position, moving-average distance and rate of change. | Reuse existing measurements. Avoid treating several correlated oversold indicators as independent confirmations. Preserve our protective risk policy. |
| [Bailey and Lopez de Prado, 2014](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) | Selecting among many trials inflates reported performance; non-normal returns also affect inference. | Record every hypothesis, threshold, combination and failed attempt. Limit trials, use later observations and account for selection when reporting uncertainty. A corrected statistic does not repair missing data or leakage. |

Our working hypothesis is that context can improve qualification. The literature does not supply a universal ATR cutoff, prove that recovery confirmation helps this roster, or guarantee that a price drop will reverse. Waiting for confirmation can improve some entries while missing the fastest rebounds. These alternatives must remain visible in the experiment.

## What the existing evidence establishes

The frozen RSI5 control requires RSI(2) below 5, stock above SMA200 and the SPY regime gate. The mean-reversion recovery control requires a qualifying stretch in the prior five sessions, followed by a completed close above the previous day's high, with stock and market gates. Its stretch is RSI(2)<10 OR three down closes OR a close below the lower band. Neither control establishes a stock-specific drawdown distribution or event-adjusted suitability.

## Five questions and measurable inputs

All features are stamped with decision time, source session, availability time, input hashes, feature version and missing-data reason. They use only information available by that decision. The following measurements are proposals to register before examining new outcomes, not implemented entry rules.

| Question | Initial measurements | Hypothesis to test and main trap |
|---|---|---|
| How unusual is this decline for this stock? | Ordered prior peak, elapsed sessions, percentage decline, decline in ATR units, trailing percentile of comparable drawdowns; existing SMA20/ATR distance as a separate stretch measurement. | Extreme deterioration may behave differently from an ordinary pullback. ATR can expand during a shock and disguise its size; preserve a pre-decline denominator alongside current volatility. |
| Is selling pressure easing? | Completed close location, downside return/range progression, new lows, volume relative to prior sessions and prior-high recovery. | Stabilization may help, or may delay entry until most of the bounce has occurred. Volume is a pressure proxy, not observed order flow. |
| Is the move shared with the market or sector? | Matched-session stock, SPY and historical sector benchmark returns; stock-minus-sector return; optionally a separately specified trailing beta-adjusted residual. | A sector selloff differs from isolated weakness. Neither residuals nor correlations establish the cause. Do not substitute SPY for a missing sector. |
| Was material information available? | Earnings timing as known then; dated earnings/guidance, financing/dilution and other classified company events; article revision, receipt and classification times. | Test known adverse-event exposure separately from technical weakness. Use observed event-risk categories, never a hindsight label of permanent repricing. |
| Does prior observed behavior help? | Counts and distributions of earlier fully matured, comparable episodes: recovery time, downside/favorable excursion and fixed-horizon net outcome. | Historical responsiveness may help qualify future setups. Sparse histories, overlapping events and selecting past winners can manufacture apparent predictability. |

Initial anatomy specification for the feature-only phase: at completed daily decision t, choose the latest occurrence of the highest high in the preceding 20 completed sessions, strictly before t, as peak p. Require C(t)<H(p) for a decline observation. Record current close drawdown `1-C(t)/H(p)`, low-so-far drawdown using lows strictly after p through t, and elapsed sessions. No low is called the final trough. Use `(H(p)-C(t))/ATR14(p-1)` as the proposed pre-decline scale; record lagged `ATR14(t-1)` separately. Missing or zero denominators produce unknown values, not zeros. A rolling peak is an operational anchor, not proof of a swing top.

For pressure diagnostics, record close location `(C-L)/(H-L)`, signed price moves `(C(t)-C(t-k))/ATR14(t-k)` for k=1 and k=3 using volatility known before each measured interval, current true range relative to `ATR14(t-1)`, and volume divided by the median of the preceding 20 sessions. A zero-range bar has unknown close location. Freeze these formulas and their adjustments together. Do not call OHLCV volume an order-book wall, buying/selling imbalance or institutional absorption.

For sector context, start with a transparent five-session stock-minus-sector return and the corresponding SPY return. Historical effective sector mapping and identical completed sessions are required. Beta estimation, if later justified, is another registered variant trained strictly before the measured decline, not an automatic extra degree of freedom.

For history, an episode identifier and its reset/overlap rules are fixed before outcome calculation. A shared event census covers every eligible symbol-session, including no-trigger days. Separate strategy candidate rows record every actual baseline setup, rejection and trigger, including those denied by capital limits. Earlier episodes enter a later decision's history only after their complete declared outcome window is available. Ongoing, missing and right-censored episodes remain explicit. No nearest-neighbor search over future episodes and no ticker-specific optimized rules. A history gate remains unavailable if the prespecified minimum independent episode count is not reached.

## Classification and decision semantics

First produce descriptive tags: ordinary/extreme relative stretch, continuing/stabilizing price pressure, shared/stock-relative decline, observed adverse-event risk, and insufficient evidence. Categories and numeric boundaries are frozen from literature-led definitions or development input distributions before held-out outcome inspection. These are observable descriptions, not causal diagnoses.

The eventual admission result has three values: qualifies, rejected with a rule reason, or unknown with an evidence reason. Unknown required inputs fail the corresponding overlay closed. A price-only control may still run under its own declared contract, so a news outage must not silently turn into a favorable no-news label. The ledger distinguishes five stages: setup opportunity, feature eligibility, qualifier verdict, portfolio admission and actual fill. Persist both the qualification result and the unchanged strategy trigger; an eligible drawdown does not itself place an order.

## Bounded experiments and validation

Keep the exact RSI5 and mean-reversion recovery plus SMA5-exit controls. For each control, propose at most five single-block overlays: depth, pressure, sector context, event context, and matured prior history. Each changes only qualification at the original decision time. Keep entry clock, ranking, exits, stops, risk, portfolio slots, capital, fees and execution policy identical to its control. A pressure overlay may accept or reject using already-completed evidence; it cannot silently wait for another bar. A later confirmation-delay experiment would need its own entry/stop timing semantics, original-to-delayed executable price comparison, and never-confirmed/missed-bounce counts. No new stop/target optimizer or risk increase in this phase.

The initial development budget is at most 120 economic treatment attempts in total. Without additional controls or retries, two controls plus ten overlay recipes would consume it: 12 recipes x five original cohorts x two frozen cost assumptions. The price-only first tranche nominally uses 60 attempts (two controls plus depth and pressure for each). The remaining capacity is conditional, not a promise to run every overlay. Coverage-matched controls, changed-execution controls, failed attempts and retries all consume this same budget; reduce overlay breadth to make room, never silently add runs or drop original cohorts. Unavailable branches are not zero-return runs. No combinations, additional thresholds or repeated folds are hidden inside this ceiling. A subsequent validation protocol must separately freeze its dates, rules and total attempt count before running; it cannot be an undeclared extension of this development search. All stages count toward the complete selection history. Run one five-stock cohort at a time; successful first-cohort engineering checks do not justify retuning against that cohort's returns.

Before execution, freeze one exact gate per overlay, minimum history counts, feature settings, coverage rules, trading clocks and source/code/config hashes. This document deliberately does not invent profitable cutoff values. A first feature-only checkpoint must return the exact proposed rules and their economic rationale before economic testing. Any choice made after seeing development outcomes is explicitly a development selection and counted as a trial.

Use two separate comparisons:

- **Paired opportunity analysis:** replay unchanged candidate entries/exits for accepted and rejected setups to quantify losses avoided and profitable opportunities discarded. Shadow opportunities are not a deployable combined portfolio. Outcome windows of one, three and five sessions are diagnostic labels, not entry features or automatic alternate exit rules.
- **Full chronological portfolio replay:** rerun the actual strategy with the gate, including freed cash, replacement admissions, partial fills and position constraints. Removing rows from old trades is not a valid portfolio backtest. Compare the same frozen candidate universe and dates with each arm's entire portfolio, never only trades that filled in both arms. Preserve the original full control. Where optional evidence is required, also run a control that applies exactly the same evidence-availability eligibility mask but no qualification rule, then compare the overlay against it. Freeze this mask from the declared information-availability contract before outcomes; it cannot depend on future losses, realized fills or active-position data gaps. Report the coverage-only control's change separately from the qualification effect. These extra controls consume the attempt budget.

Missing observations during any required outcome interval make that result unavailable. Do not drop only the troublesome losing or open positions. An overlay avoiding the date of a control's failure cannot establish an all-cohort improvement over that incomplete control.

Temporal partitions are frozen from an access-and-coverage inventory. The viewed April-October period is development. Earlier/later data must be checked for previous inspection, not assumed untouched. Use chronological training then validation; reserve one final contiguous holdout for a frozen candidate and open it once. Remove training events whose label or position intervals overlap validation/holdout; insert an embargo covering the longest actual configured outcome/position horizon and availability delay. Read-only warmup bars may precede a partition, but future outcome labels may not. Do not assume five sessions is sufficient if the execution clock carries exposure longer.

Use joint calendar blocks when estimating uncertainty to preserve market/sector co-movement, and cluster repeated observations of the same drawdown. Cohorts are operational batches, not five independent markets. No randomly shuffled train/test split. Multiple testing includes prior cycles and rejected variants, not only this plan's surviving recipes. Statistical analysis remains separate by strategy/cohort; no pooled return table. Rules and thresholds remain fixed throughout a validation partition; monthly reporting does not authorize monthly retuning. A future online update algorithm requires its own freeze and mature-label timing tests.

## How we judge improvement

Publish each month separately by exact strategy/version/hash, cohort and cost assumption. Show net marked-to-market return, difference from the matching control, monthly close drawdown, exposure, trades, non-fills, fees, holding duration, accepted/rejected/unknown counts and concentration. Flag partial months. Report sampled intraday adverse excursions separately; daily-close drawdown cannot stand in for intraday risk.

Report rejected profitable opportunities alongside losses avoided, and uncertainty around those estimates. Zero trades is inactivity, not consistency. A higher win rate with lower monthly portfolio returns is not automatically an improvement. A lower drawdown obtained mostly by sitting in cash must be accompanied by exposure and opportunity cost.

Before validation, preregister the required paired incremental monthly net-return effect at unchanged risk, acceptable monthly drawdown tradeoff, minimum activity/exposure, and precision/sample requirements based on development variability and existing risk limits. Report raw opportunity counts, unique episode and issuer counts, and overlap, not only closed trades; ten trades is an exploratory flag, not statistical proof. At minimum, an overlay must survive the adverse cost assumption, avoid dependence on one issuer or month, and show the same useful direction across later periods and sufficiently supported cohorts. If uncertainty cannot distinguish improvement from noise, retain research status. Do not improvise passing thresholds after opening validation.

The 25-symbol diagnostic roster cannot satisfy the binding promotion requirement of at least 30 distinct holdout issuers. Existing requirements for 60 independent formations, 12 untouched holdout formations, valid historical membership and other gates also remain. This plan does not authorize expanding the user's wishlist or weakening those requirements. Any broader promotion study needs its own approved universe/protocol.
