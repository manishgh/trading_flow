## Decisions needed before the cohort feature is implemented

1. **Membership:** choose automatic grouping by a documented stable order, explicitly named groups, or both. Show the actual symbols in every group before starting. Decide how the final smaller group is handled; do not drop leftover stocks silently.
2. **Capital:** configure starting capital for each strategy/cohort portfolio. If amounts can differ by cohort, show them in the same preview. A shared source stream never shares capital.
3. **Daily rule:** decide whether to enforce a maximum of new distinct stock symbols entered per New York trading day. Select the exact cap; two or three is not automatically chosen. Define whether only actual entry fills consume it and whether same-symbol reentry counts again.
4. **Open-position rule:** choose how many holdings may remain open simultaneously across days within one portfolio. This is independent of the daily new-entry rule.
5. **Display:** confirm monthly results by exact strategy and cohort, open-position valuation versus realized results, and explicit partial/unavailable months. Do not display accumulated or pooled returns.
6. **Resource budget:** approve run count, requested period, required data and bounded execution scheduling before the matrix starts. Do not generate every combination and decide the budget afterward.

These questions describe the original 19 October review. Membership, independent capital, paper/live defaults and monthly display have since been answered; see 07. Only the exact operational counting/scope proposal and detailed implementation design remain for review. Do not repeat resolved questions or add controls when existing strategy/run fields express the behavior.
