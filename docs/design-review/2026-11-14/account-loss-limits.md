# Paper account loss limits

The paper defaults are 2% daily, 4% weekly and 10% peak to trough. They apply to marked account equity, including realized and unrealized gains and losses. These thresholds are the selected policy for this paper account. Live routing stays disabled.

| Limit | Production configuration key | Environment variable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | `TradingFlow:Production:max_daily_loss_pct` | `TRADINGFLOW_MAX_DAILY_LOSS_PCT` |
| Weekly | `TradingFlow:Production:max_weekly_loss_pct` | `TRADINGFLOW_MAX_WEEKLY_LOSS_PCT` |
| Peak to trough | `TradingFlow:Production:max_peak_to_trough_loss_pct` | `TRADINGFLOW_MAX_PEAK_TO_TROUGH_LOSS_PCT` |

The daily reference is the verified prior exchange-session close. The weekly reference is the final close of the previous New York exchange week. Daily and weekly stops last through their periods even if equity recovers. The peak starts at the verified prior-week close when the account guard first initializes. It accumulates observed marks and confirmed intervening closes. An intraday peak during a supervision gap can be missed. A peak breach has no automatic reset. Funding changes with an unproved equity basis also require review. An administrator-reviewed peak reset and bounded funding rebaseline are now implemented locally; the review workflow is described below. Deployment remains separate.

Alpaca returns current equity, prior equity, and non-trade adjustments in one account response. The archived calendar and portfolio history supply the previous session closes and completed-session cash-flow series. The guard compares prior equity at portfolio history's cent precision and uses the more precise account figure for the daily reference. Incomplete history, stale observations, missing or nonzero current non-trade adjustments, and unresolved funding changes all refuse new entries. [Alpaca account fields](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/getaccount-1) · [Alpaca portfolio history](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/getaccountportfoliohistory-1)

The evidence contract checks reported cash flows and the current non-trade adjustment field. It does not independently reconcile in-kind securities transfers, securities journals, or noncash corporate actions. Before relying on these limits after such activity, review the underlying activity evidence and confirm the account's equity basis. Reconciliation of those noncash activities remains outside this implementation; the review action refuses unsupported or undated funding evidence. [Alpaca account activity types](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/getaccountactivitiesbyactivitytype-1)

The journal uses the broker, account and execution mode as its key. Changing a strategy profile or restarting a worker leaves the same account risk state in effect. The existing entry path checks state during reservation and again in the SQLite transaction preceding the first broker POST. Breaches and unresolved cancellations are recorded in `risk_events`. Orders already known to the broker can be adopted, and reconciliation, protective orders and exits continue.

Recording a breach also records a pending broker-order sweep in the same transaction. It survives any daily or weekly reset and keeps entries closed until a fresh broker snapshot and the journal account for existing opening work. An earlier dispatch attempt with an unresolved broker outcome keeps the sweep pending. A newer breach starts a new sweep.

On a breach, reconciliation requests cancellation of proven standalone opening remainders, including partially filled standalone orders. The command and crash-recovery paths recheck exact account, parent and group evidence before DELETE. Linked or unknown groups are preserved with an explicit pending review; a linked remainder can still fill while that review remains open. Terminal parent status alone cannot clear it: cumulative fills must be accounted in the journal, repeated fresh broker positions must agree with the ledger, and the remaining exposure must have exact owned resting stop coverage or be confirmed flat. Pending, replaced, done_for_day, missing, duplicated or contradictory evidence does not release the entry stop. Fully filled native bracket/OTO protection is recognized from exact current parent/leg and position-generation evidence; uncertain native stops never justify a competing backstop. Shutdown retains linked groups and completes its protection check before reporting unresolved work. Alpaca does not document an atomic cancellation/protection handoff for racing linked fills, so unconditional RSK-02 remains open for these groups. [Alpaca bracket orders](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca#bracket-orders)

Existing historical saved plans have no frozen setting for this account policy. The pure calculation can use frozen simulated equity evidence when that option is designed. Historical results are untouched.


## Reviewed reset workflow (15 November implementation)

Operations links administrators to Review paper account loss limits. The form and authenticated API GET /api/operations/account-loss/ show the exact immutable journal revision. POST /api/operations/account-loss/review accepts expectedRevision, action (reset_peak or rebaseline_funding) and a nonblank reason of at most 2000 characters. Actor identity comes from the signed-in administrator; account and equity come from the configured paper broker. Both mutation routes require antiforgery and are refused while runtime is paused.

A reset fetches fresh archived broker evidence, then checks the entire stream revision and outstanding opening work in the same SQLite writer transaction that appends the reset and risk audit. A changed observation, cancellation or sweep invalidates an old review. Prior events are preserved. Repeating an old request cannot perform another reset. A peak reset starts a new observed epoch from positive current equity, while same-period daily/weekly latches and missed current-week losses remain enforced. A newly found loss starts the normal pending order sweep.

Funding rebaseline additionally requires a broker-supported latest transfer date and both verified reference closes strictly after that date. This can require waiting until the next verified week. Current non-trade adjustments must be zero; unknown/noncash attribution stays refused. The action also starts a new observed peak. A later transfer triggers a new funding stop. No user-entered equity, fabricated cash-flow adjustment, or general unlock of reconciliation/protection controls exists.

Verification: 268 combined paper tests passed, including SQLite revision/cancellation interleaving and restart, two missed-weekly-loss replay cases, funding anchor timing, administrator/CSRF/principal checks. Separate plan/execution and code/design reviewers signed off. This local implementation has not reset any operator account or been deployed. RSK-02 linked-parent cancellation remains partial.
