# TradingFlow: daily workflow and optional chart indicators

15 November 2026. Concrete proposal for review; these UI and indicator additions are not installed yet.

## What the review found

The shared chart, stock links, monthly results and preparation evidence provide a useful foundation. The biggest usability problem is that the screen does not put the next useful action first. A new operator has to understand internal runtime state and search below a large table before starting preparation.

Read-only browser review used the signed-in local app on port 10088, deployed build `91dd9e1`. It opened Desk, an existing preparation result, Strategy Lab and the existing sector comparison from saved plan 192. It did not submit orders, create experiments, collect a new preparation revision or start a backtest. Source review checked the corresponding pages, preparation worker registration and shared chart services. The final Operations navigation timed out in the browser; no additional Operations visual verification is claimed.

## Everyday flow

The proposed Desk order is **workspace status → preparation → stock list and selected chart → strategy-run summary**. News and earnings remain available in the selected-stock context and their dedicated pages. Preparation is also reachable directly; operators should not have to scroll through 30 stocks to find it.

For example, on Monday before the open the preparation card could say: “Monday 16 November · Evening evidence available · Morning collection needed · Preparation worker paused”. Its useful action is **Review saved evidence**, with a link explaining worker state. It must not offer a misleading **Run now** action while the worker cannot execute. Once a permitted worker completes morning collection, the same card offers **Review morning list**. A reviewed list opens Desk with its source revision visible. Selecting a research strategy displays checks; it does not authorize a paper run.

Strategy Lab should answer four questions in order: **What rule am I testing? Which stocks and dates? What execution and capital assumptions? What exactly will run?** This ordering is a UI change: no new required persisted question/horizon fields and no mutation of earlier plans or records. Existing services still freeze the saved plan and determine costs, cash, coverage and publication. The UI only makes those choices easier to inspect. Results open at a selected month and preserve separate strategy/version/group capital; no pooled or accumulated return headline is introduced.

The UI must show the selected run’s actual decision. The default Lab currently says `RETAIN_RESEARCH`, while the inspected plan 192 result says `FAILED_GATE`. Neither label is authorization for paper trading.

## Optional chart library

Reuse the installed `Skender.Stock.Indicators` 2.7.1 calculations through the C# engine and the existing TradingView Lightweight Charts renderer. No new plotting or numerical dependency is proposed. These displays describe observed data; toggling them cannot select a strategy, change risk settings, subscribe a ticker to trading or submit an order.

The picker groups indicators and explains units and source interval. Each selection has a short description, a remove control and an unavailable reason when input evidence is insufficient.

| Group | Proposed ready-made choices | Display |
|---|---|---|
| Trend | SMA 20/50/200; EMA 9/20/50/200 | Price lines; fixed presets, source interval always shown |
| Momentum | RSI 14; MACD 12/26/9; ADX 14 with +DI/-DI | Optional lower panes; reference lines are descriptive |
| Volatility | ATR 14, ATR percent; Bollinger Bands 20/2 | ATR readout or pane; bands on price |
| Volume | Existing volume bars and RTH VWAP; OBV; verified same-time relative volume | Volume pane, cumulative OBV pane or labelled RVOL readout |
| Structure | Existing confirmed zones, prior day/week levels; daily zone horizons over week/month/year on intraday candles | Labelled bands, source interval and confirmation time, accessible level list |

EMA 9 is a requested chart preset, not a currently computed fixed field: use the same pinned generic EMA calculation and test its seed behavior. Other families already exist in the engine, but exposing a full historical series still needs a reusable calculation contract and verification. The library should not call strategy evaluation just to obtain indicator values.

Default remains candles, volume and selected structural context. Offer **Intraday** (RTH VWAP, EMA20, ATR readout), **Swing** (daily SMA50/200, RSI14, ATR readout) and **Custom** presets. Intraday RVOL appears only when its 20-prior-session evidence is verified. A preset changes chart display only. Cap all additional lower panes at two (including ATR and OBV) and price studies at four; users can replace a study rather than compressing the candles indefinitely. Mobile initially shows one oscillator pane, with accessible switching to the other selected pane.

“EMA20 · 5m” means 20 five-minute observations. “SMA200 · 1d” remains a daily calculation even when drawn on five-minute candles. A 5m chart can show daily confirmed zones from week, month or year lookback, labelled “1d structure · year lookback”. This does not claim that monthly or yearly candle pivots were calculated. Display interval, structural source and lookback are separate controls. Preserve the current-only zone contract: use the current band geometry and actual effective time in the right-hand labelled band/list, retain the selected context across paging, and do not paint historical zone segments. Current merged bands must not be painted at an older pivot time. Versioned historical geometry remains a separate design. Daily levels or indicators on intraday candles join only at the completed source observation's availability, never at the start of that day.

Weekly candles/weekly pivots, forming-candle previews, authenticated entry/stop/target/fill markers and advanced anchored VWAP/volume profile remain separate later milestones. The existing news/earnings markers stay available. In particular, volume at a price cannot be truthfully inferred from whole-candle volume. This proposal does not silently replace those larger data and permission designs with approximate signals.

## Calculation, caching and limits

Keep the current on-demand common chart service: no bulk chart creation for research candidates and no new provider subscription merely because a chart opens. Reuse stored Alpaca SIP candles and the existing bounded history loader. Retain four connections per operator by default, 128 total, 20 selection/history requests per operator per minute, 32 history cache entries, a 500-bar visible page and the current source acquisition ceilings (500 daily inputs, 16,000 minute inputs and 15 intraday sessions).

Indicators are requested by a finite allowlist of names and preset parameters. Share results across viewers by canonical symbol, source interval, session, adjustment basis, formula version, source revision and fixed calculation anchor. Bound the derived cache to one latest result per active history entry/profile, with at most four profiles per entry and deterministic eviction. Also cap derived output and checkpoint storage globally at 100,000 sample points and 32 MiB accounted allocation, whichever is reached first; use compact typed arrays with conservative object overhead accounting and deterministic eviction. Permit at most two concurrent numerical calculations with at most 32 queued distinct requests, coalescing identical work; overload returns a retryable reason. Canceled viewers release their own work and do not cancel shared work still required by another viewer. Avoid retaining a full new copy for every browser update. Disabled families do not fetch history or calculate outputs.

Warm-up is separate from visible history. Verify the pinned package’s seed and convergence behavior before exposing each family. With a 500-daily-input ceiling, the left side of a long MACD or ATR chart may have no sufficiently warmed result. Show that shorter available suffix and explain why. The 15-session intraday acquisition limit does not satisfy a 20-prior-session RVOL baseline: reuse already verified compatible evidence if available; otherwise show “Insufficient baseline”. The offered RVOL is cumulative RTH volume divided by the median cumulative volume at the matching completed session time across exactly 20 verified compatible prior sessions, following the existing evidence profile. Missing counterparts or a zero denominator make it unavailable; slot RVOL is a different, unoffered measure in this checkpoint. Do not quietly expand downloads or substitute a last-N-bars ratio. Fifteen complete regular sessions supply at most 390 fifteen-minute observations before early closes and gaps; EMA200 may have no sufficiently warmed suffix. Before shipping, record a pinned per-family/interval table of required seed, convergence and available plotted observations, and display unavailability when any requirement is unmet.

Define each numerical series by canonical source identity, formula profile, source revision and a fixed seed anchor/checkpoint independent of the viewport and moving history cursor. Preserve that identity across reconnect/cache eviction or mark the affected series unavailable; an older page before the verified seed must show unavailable or a separately labelled anchored segment rather than reseeding the existing series. Use a stable calculation anchor/checkpoint across visible-page changes. Loading earlier candles must not reseed an existing EMA and move already displayed values. Corrections invalidate dependent results and recompute the affected suffix. Show reconstructed history as reconstructed; do not claim that today’s corrected/adjusted values were known at a past decision. Missing observations remain gaps, and zero-volume/no-trade evidence must remain distinct from failed acquisition. Values carry source interval, formula parameters, adjustment basis, cutoff, availability and warm-up status. Use the sessions, adjustment, gap and degenerate-value requirements in the companion optional-chart-indicators-research.md contract; also test ADX flat/zero-range behavior and OBV equal-close and zero-volume behavior against explicit expected fixtures. No infinities, invented zeros or implicit forward filling are allowed.

## Reuse and affected files

Workspace flow changes use `Pages/Shared/_Layout.cshtml`, `Pages/Desk.cshtml`, `Pages/Shared/_PreparationWorkspace.cshtml`, `Pages/Preparation.cshtml`, the existing Desk/preparation JavaScript and CSS, and the existing Operations health service/API. Add explicit preparation-worker availability to that health contract if it is absent; do not infer it from whether a button rendered. The same server runtime capability must guard manual and curated collection before any occurrence or job is created; disabled or unknown availability returns a specific reason. Scheduling controls distinguish a saved schedule setting from an executing worker. Preserve read-only review/history, save-paused and navigation. Avoid another independent health poll per panel: share one bounded status request and expose its age/failure state.

Lab improvements use `Pages/Backtests.cshtml`, its page model and existing saved-plan/result services. Preserve the preview/save/start contracts and original research evidence. Missing-result presentation is read-only until the exact artifact and identity have been located.

Chart additions use `Services/Charts/ChartSeriesService.cs`, `ChartHistorySource.cs`, the chart contracts, reusable pure indicator functions in `TradingFlow.Engine/Indicators`, `_StockChart.cshtml`, `stock-chart.js`, `stock-chart-plot.js` and chart CSS. The Web layer requests calculations and formats responses; the engine owns formulas. Carry forward chart CanRead authorization, cursor ownership and shared request limits on every path including cache hits. Only public market-data calculations may be shared across viewers; account/run marker payloads must never enter that shared cache. Selection preferences may be stored on this browser with a versioned key and a reset action. Keep the existing 256 KiB HTTP/WebSocket snapshot ceiling. Represent indicator samples compactly against shared bar times with series-level metadata, and validate the serialized maximum selection before sending; an oversized selection must return an explicit bounded-response reason rather than silently dropping the chart. No account database migration is needed for chart preferences. The current chart response may evolve directly because this is a preproduction product; no legacy format adapter is proposed.

## Execution and acceptance

1. **Workflow clarity:** status strip, preparation visibility/worker gating, honest feed states, simpler Desk and Lab layout, missing-artifact presentation. Independently review plan/execution and code/design. Check paused/active/unknown states, correct exit affordances, keyboard operation and desktop/phone layouts.
2. **Chart library and multi-horizon context:** implement the finite optional studies and shared calculation contract. Validate pinned formulas against independent expected examples; cover flat/one-sided prices, gaps, zero volume, split basis, early closes/DST, insufficient warm-up, correction replay and viewport invariance. Check higher-context confirmation causality, two-pane limits, maximum 500-bar payload serialization within 256 KiB, existing WebSocket message framing and reconnect, rapid symbol switching, reconnect and shared caching.
3. **Real-data acceptance and guide update:** use existing retained candles first; capture source/basis/cutoff and visible values for intraday and daily examples, including unavailable RVOL. Verify browser output against server calculations. Measure memory and update latency with four tabs per operator, high fanout up to the existing 128-connection ceiling, cancellations, maximum profiles and configured history; reject unbounded growth or duplicate acquisitions. Against the same already-warm history, indicator toggles and four identical tabs must cause zero additional upstream history acquisitions; disabled families must have zero calculation calls, and derived-cache variants must remain at most four per history entry. Count ATR and OBV panes within the same two-lower-pane cap. Use focused tests for components and required full release checks before updating the running app. No new economic experiment or broker execution is included.

This is three bounded implementation checkpoints, not a full navigation rewrite. Existing risk and execution rules remain authoritative. Each checkpoint must have different independent A plan/execution and B code/design reviewers, with findings and resolutions recorded in the implementation plan. Update the how-to guide after observed UI changes, and commit locally without GitHub sync.

Market-open paper submission, fills and broker protection still require their separately recorded operational acceptance. That remaining task is not satisfied by a usable chart or passing synthetic tests.
